SAMPLE DATASET: A Christmas Carol (Charles Dickens, 1843, public domain)

This is a placeholder text file used for GraphRAG dataset registration testing.
It represents the classic Victorian novella about Ebenezer Scrooge's transformation.

The story follows a miserly old man who is visited by three spirits on Christmas Eve.
Through visions of past, present, and future, he learns the value of generosity and compassion.
By morning, Scrooge becomes a changed person, embracing the holiday spirit and helping those in need.

Key characters: Ebenezer Scrooge, Bob Cratchit, Tiny Tim, Jacob Marley,
Ghost of Christmas Past, Ghost of Christmas Present, Ghost of Christmas Yet to Come.

Themes: redemption, generosity, social responsibility, the spirit of Christmas.

This sample file is checked into the repository for deterministic local dev/test use.
