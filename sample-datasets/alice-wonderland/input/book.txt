SAMPLE DATASET: Alice in Wonderland (Lewis Carroll, 1865, public domain)

This is a placeholder text file used for GraphRAG dataset registration testing.
It represents the classic fantasy novel about a girl's adventures in a surreal underground world.

The story begins when Alice follows a White Rabbit down a hole and enters Wonderland.
She encounters a series of strange characters and nonsensical situations.
Along the way she changes size multiple times, attends a mad tea party, and plays croquet.
The tale culminates in a chaotic trial before Alice wakes up and realizes it was all a dream.

Key characters: Alice, White Rabbit, Cheshire Cat, Mad Hatter, March Hare,
Queen of Hearts, King of Hearts, Caterpillar, Mock Turtle, Dormouse.

Themes: curiosity, identity, logic vs nonsense, growing up, the nature of reality.

This sample file is checked into the repository for deterministic local dev/test use.
