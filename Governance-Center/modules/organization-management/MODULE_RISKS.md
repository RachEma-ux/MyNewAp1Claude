# MODULE_RISKS — Organization Management

## Purpose

Defines the risk register for OM.

## Key Risks

### R1 — Structural duplication across modules
- HR continues to own org structure
- OM introduced without clear migration

Impact: High
Mitigation: enforce ownership doctrine and migration plan

### R2 — Authority chain inconsistency
- reporting relationships incomplete or incorrect

Impact: Critical
Mitigation: validation + periodic checks

### R3 — Position-state inconsistency
- filled/vacant/frozen states not aligned with reality

Impact: High
Mitigation: strict lifecycle enforcement

### R4 — Hidden authority in PM or HR
- PM or HR bypass OM authority chain

Impact: Critical
Mitigation: enforce approval through OM-derived chain

### R5 — Weak audit coverage
- structural changes not logged

Impact: High
Mitigation: enforce audit model

### R6 — Migration risk
- moving structure from HR to OM breaks existing flows

Impact: Medium
Mitigation: staged migration strategy

## Current Status

All risks are pre-runtime and must be considered before implementation.
