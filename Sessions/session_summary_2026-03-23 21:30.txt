## Changes Made (commit fa445f3)

### 1. Critical Async Bug Fix — Compensation & Relations Routers
- **Problem**: All `checkHrAccess()` calls in compensation/router.ts (17 calls) and relations/router.ts (16 calls) were missing `await`. Since `checkHrAccess` is async (queries hr_role_assignments table), the permission check was fire-and-forget — it started but never blocked, meaning ANY user could access protected endpoints.
- **Fix**: Added `await` to every `checkHrAccess` call in both routers.

### 2. Mutation Permission Enforcement — Compensation & Relations Routers
- **Problem**: Mutation endpoints used `checkHrAccess()` (read-check pattern) instead of `requireHrPermission()` (write-check pattern). This is semantically wrong — `checkHrAccess` returns masking info, `requireHrPermission` throws FORBIDDEN.
- **Fix**: Upgraded all mutation permission checks:
  - Compensation: 10 mutations → `requireHrPermission` with COMPENSATION_MANAGE, COMPENSATION_WRITE, BENEFITS_MANAGE, BENEFITS_WRITE
  - Relations: 9 mutations → `requireHrPermission` with POLICY_MANAGE, POLICY_WRITE, RELATIONS_WRITE, RELATIONS_MANAGE

### 3. Self-Approval Prevention (Segregation of Duties)
Applied `preventSelfApproval()` to 5 sensitive approval paths:
- **Bonus approval** (compensation/transitionBonusRecord) — prevents approving own bonus
- **Leave approval** (time/updateLeaveRequestStatus) — prevents approving own leave
- **Overtime approval** (time/updateOvertimeRequestStatus) — prevents approving own OT
- **Time entry approval** (time/updateTimeEntry) — prevents approving own timesheet
- **Performance review** (performance/submitManagerReview) — prevents reviewing yourself

### 4. Missing Permission Checks — Time Router
