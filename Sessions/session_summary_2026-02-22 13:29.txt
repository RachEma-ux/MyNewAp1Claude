### 5. Candidate Pipeline Stages (Major Feature)
Implemented full pipeline: Register → Validate → Publish → Catalog

**Register tab** (Candidate page):
- Shows entries with `reviewState === "needs_review"` + tag "candidate"
- Admin clicks "Register" button → approves entry, moves to Validate

**Validate tab** (Candidate page):
- Shows entries with `reviewState === "approved"` + not yet validated
- Admin runs validation

**Publish tab** (Candidate page):
- Shows validated entries (`validationStatus === "passed"`) not yet active
- Admin publishes

**Catalog tab** (Manage page):
- ONLY shows entries tagged "candidate" AND `status === "active"`
- Empty state directs to Candidate pipeline
- No direct creation — only source is published entries from Candidate
