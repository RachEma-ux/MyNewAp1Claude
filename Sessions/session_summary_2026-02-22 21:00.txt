## Architecture Decisions
- Scorecard engine is pure-functional (stateless runners, no side effects beyond reporting)
- Evidence bundles use SHA-256 for integrity verification
- In-memory scorecard history (max 50 entries) for quick access; CI artifacts for persistent storage
- Stage gate thresholds: Register (no Critical), Validate (no Critical/High, score>=50), Publish (no Critical/High/Medium, score>=80), Catalog (score=100)
- Drift detection runs on configurable interval (default 15 min) with score drop threshold escalation
