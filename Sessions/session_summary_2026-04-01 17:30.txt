## Architecture Insight
The WF Sandbox Maestro chat does NOT use the SSE `/api/catalog/:id/invoke/stream` endpoint.
It uses tRPC mutation `sandboxWf.maestro.roundTable` → `executeRoundTable()` in
`server/sandbox-wf/service.ts` → which calls `invokeCatalogEntry()` from `server/catalog/invoke.ts`.
The invoke function dispatches to `executeCatalogChatStream()` based on runtime kind (llm-chat).
